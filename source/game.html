Release movement keys to slow into a hover. Red columns show anti-air coverage; the amber dome marks tank defense. Both trigger gunfire after 0.8 seconds. Red columns extend into the sky layer, so climbing above a vehicle does not find a safe gap. The amber grid starts sky defense at 27 / 26 / 24 m by challenge: descend before its separate 2-second meter fills. Leaving a vehicle's radius resets its lock. Launched missiles keep tracking; solid buildings can intercept them. Contact with the tank succeeds at any speed.
