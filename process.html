Human–AI development timeline

What changed, who decided, and what remains uncertain.

This page is a concise view of the chronological development log. It records design constraints, implementation choices, tests, and unresolved questions without smoothing over the process.

INTERACTION 0108 SEP 2026

FIRST PLAYABLE

Core loop and project space

The student confirmed Last-Dash.md as the source of truth and authorized the agent to resolve underspecified mechanics in service of accurate learning and complete playability.

  • Built the website and playable game in the same milestone.
  • Preserved the speed–maneuverability trade-off and three spatial challenge presets.
  • Browser testing exposed a target-path field mismatch; it was corrected and the rebuilt moving system passed the follow-up console check.
  • Used the separate logging protocol as the authoritative log format.
INTERACTION 0208 SEP 2026

CONTROL REVISION

From aircraft steering to six-axis drone flight

The student rejected automatic forward motion and specified a hover-capable control model: independent translation, full mouse orientation, and explicit barrel roll.

  • Removed minimum forward speed and automatic travel.
  • Separated forward/backward, lateral, and vertical acceleration from 360° body orientation.
  • Updated every player-facing control reference and preserved velocity-dependent rotational authority.
INTERACTION 0308 SEP 2026

INPUT CORRECTION

Roll direction matched to the control intent

The student clarified the desired roll direction. The rotation sign and every control reference were corrected so Q rolls left and E rolls right.

INTERACTION 0408 SEP 2026

THREAT TUNING

Denser defenses and faster consequences

Every challenge gained more defense vehicles and larger threat zones. Continuous exposure now completes lock in 0.8 seconds, mouse sensitivity is 30% lower, and result titles use three unambiguous outcomes.

INTERACTION 0508 SEP 2026

MODELS & COMBAT FEEDBACK

Readable attacks in a richer airspace

The student supplied FBX assets and requested skyline missile defense, visible anti-air gunfire, equal 0.8-second tank defense, unrestricted impact speed, and particle destruction.

  • Replaced procedural vehicles and buildings with the supplied geometry; converted and shared meshes for browser use.
  • Added a separate 2-second high-altitude lock, tracking missiles, smoke trails, and visible cannon rounds.
  • Removed the minimum impact speed: fast approaches reduce defense exposure instead.
  • Added model breakup, sparks, smoke, muzzle flashes, rotor wash, and a delayed result window.
INTERACTION 0608 SEP 2026

COVERAGE TUNING

Close the safe altitude gap

The student asked for wider anti-air zones and a lower sky layer. Vehicle radii increased by about 20%, and the sky threshold dropped to 27 / 26 / 24 m. Red cylindrical zones overlap it by four metres, removing the safe space above the old hemisphere edges.
